Booking Policy
How Booking Works Submit your booking online or call (484) 999-0194. After you submit, you'll receive an email copy of your request. We review each request and send a confirmation with your appointment details — including date, time, and final pricing.
Confirmations Your appointment is confirmed once you receive our email confirmation. If you don't receive it within 24 hours, check your spam folder or call us directly.
Cancellations & Rescheduling Life happens. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please contact us as soon as possible so we can adjust our schedule. Reach us at (484) 999-0194.
Pricing Policy
Starting Prices All prices listed on this website are starting prices. Final pricing depends on your vehicle's size and condition. We'll always confirm the exact price before we begin work.
Vehicle Size Coupes and sedans are the baseline for our starting prices. SUVs, trucks, vans, and larger vehicles are priced higher. Call for an exact quote for your vehicle.
Condition-Based Pricing Vehicles in heavily soiled condition, with significant staining, or with excessive pet hair may require additional time and materials. Any upcharges are communicated upfront.
Maintenance Plans Maintenance plans (bi-weekly, monthly, bi-monthly) are available only after a Full Detail has been completed. Plan pricing starts at the rates listed and may vary by vehicle size.
